1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files that the browser stores on the device. A page can also use similar technologies, such as localStorageSome technologies are necessary for security and basic functionality, while analytical and marketing technologies require the right choice of users.

2. Our categories

Required settings

These settings allow you to work in a site, in order to make sure your privacy is safe and secure. They are not used to create a marketing profile and cannot be excluded through our panel because they are necessary for the basic function.

Analysis and measurement

If you accept the analysis, Google Analytics 4 can set up first-page cookies to distinguish users and sessions, measuring visited pages, devices, visitors' sources and content effects. The data is used to improve the site and report.

Advertising and personalisation
