About us

Digital Agency Building The presence he's selling.

Digital X was born of simple frustration: too much business in BiH pays for web and marketing that looks good and does not bring questions. We do the opposite — from goal and measurable result, then back to design.

Why We Exist

The result before the aesthetics.

The website, campaign or automation is not the goal for us — the goal is more questions, more sales and less handiwork. Therefore, each project begins with the question "what should this achieve?" and not "how should it look?".

We work with small and medium-sized businesses in Bosnia and Herzegovina, the region and diaspora (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) — salons, offices, crafts, shops and utility companies.

01Web & e-commerce — pages and shops that are quickly loaded and leading to a query or purchase.
02SEO / Geo / AEO — visibility on Google, maps and AI browsers such as ChatGPT and Perplexity.
03& Contents — campaigns and content measured by one number: return.
04AI Automation — chatbots, reminders and workflows that remove manual copying.
