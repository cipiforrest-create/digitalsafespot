AI Business automation: what can be automated in small business

Guide · AI automation · July 2026.

Most small business owners spend 10-30 hours a month on the jobs that the machine does better: recall, rewrite, send, copy from table to table. Automation in 2026 is no longer a luxury corporation — set up in a week and costs less than one working day of the employee.

Six automations that are fast to pay

  1. AI chatbot (of 490 KM + 149 KM/mj): answers 80% of customer questions 24/7 — Detailed Guide Here.
  2. Reminders for terms (of 349 KM + 249 KM/mj): Customer orders online, system itself sends WhatsApp/SMS reminder the day before. Salons and offices with this typically halves missed terms.
  3. Review collector (of 199 KM + 249 KM/mj): automatically post-service message with Google review link — Guide here.
  4. Email sequence (from 249 KM): The new query automatically receives a series of messages that lead to purchase. The leader who gets the answer in the first minute is worth more than the one who waits until tomorrow.
  5. Auto reports: The first of the month comes my own review: visits, pollinations, sales — in human language, no tool - digging.
  6. Internal processes per measure: The offer itself agrees with the price list, the invoice is sent, the table is filled out. The biggest savings are here — and the biggest surprises as much as possible.

What introduction looks like

Without revolution: (1) choose one The process that hurts most, (2) set automation in 5-7 days, (3) measure savings for a month, (4) then spread further. The automation that works quietly and confidently is worth more than ten "impressive" that no one uses.

Real Math

A worker who manually sends reminders, overwrites queries and reports spends ~15 hours a month. At 15 KM/h it is 225 KM every month — forever. Automation: one-time from 199-490 KM + ~30 KM per month. It pays for the first month and does not ask for an annual fee.
