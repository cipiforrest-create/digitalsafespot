Local SEO: how to be first on Google map in your city

Guide · GEO / local visibility · July 2026.

When someone in your town type in a "tooth near" or "car service + city", Google above all results shows three business on a mapThese three get a huge majority of calls, all the others are crumbs. Local SEO (GEO) is the discipline of entry at these three.

What Google sees when choosing "top 3"

  1. Google Business Profile: Full-full — exact primary category, description, working time, price services, at least 10 real photos. Empty profile = invisible profile.
  2. Reviews: number, average, freshness and — what most do not know — text review. "Great hairdresser, fast order, Sarajevo" is worth more than the "5 stars".
  3. NAP consistency: The name, address and phone must be identical on the site, profile, Facebook and in folders. Google reads different data as unreliable.
  4. Activity: response to reviews, weekly posts on the profile, up-to-date information.

Why this is the most profitable marketing for local business

The person who's looking for "near" is ready to buy today — it is the most valuable visitor possible, and click from the map is free. Unlike ads that stand when the budget stops, the profile is arranged for months. In smaller cities of BiH, competition is so inactive that it is real-life with two or three months of discipline.
