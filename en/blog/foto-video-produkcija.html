Photo and video production: why amateur photos cost customers

Guide · Production · July 2026.

The palace decides in half a second. On Instagram, on a web shop, on Google profile — the customer first sees the picture and then reads. The blurry photo of the kebab under the neon and the professional frame of the same kebab are not the same product in the eyes of the customer, although the kebab is the same.

What are we doing?

  • Product photo: for webshops and catalogues – clean, consistent, in your brand. Directly raises the conversion in the store.
  • Space and team: The local, office, salon, people — photos for the site, Google profile and networks that build trust before the first arrival.
  • Events: openings, conferences, promotions — covered for both memory and marketing.
  • Reels/TikProcess production: script → recording → editing with titles and trending sound. Short video is the format that algorithms currently most award.

What a shooting term looks like

  1. Preparation: we agree on what is being recorded and for which channel (shopping site?) — each platform is looking for its own format.
  2. Recording (2-4h): We come with equipment and light; you do your work; we catch the staff.
  3. Processing and delivery: processed photos + video clips cut out for each platform (16:9, 9:16, square).
