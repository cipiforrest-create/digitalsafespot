How much does a website cost in BiH in 2026? A real guide through prices

Guide · Web · Updated July 2026.

Short answer: The professional presentation page in BiH costs 500–1,800 KM, a 3,000 KM-up web shop and a 5,000 KM-to-35,000+ KM app. The difference in price is not "how much to charge" – the difference is what is involved.

What do you get for a few digits?

  • Up to 500 KM: simple presentation page (up to 5 pages, contact form, mobile design). For lawyers, offices, crafts and salons this is quite enough — if is done properly: quickly, safely and with the basic SEO.
  • 800-1,800 KM: larger pages with more content, multilinguality, blog, more advanced SEO preparation, custom design.
  • Of a capacity of 3000 KM: webshop — products, payment, delivery, accounts. This is where you pay and set up the process, not just appearance.
  • Of 5000 KM: the use according to the measure (reservation systems, internal records, portals).

Five questions you ask everyone

  1. How fast is the page loaded on the phone? You are looking for Google Rating 85+. Slow pages lose up to 20% of visitors per second of waiting.
  2. Is SEO on or being charged? A site without a basic SEO is a window in the basement.
  3. Who owns the site and the domain? Always you. If the agency retains ownership, run.
  4. What happens after the launch? Without update and backup, the site is a matter of time. Maintenance costs 29-150 KM per month.
  5. Can I change the contents myself? If you pay the programmer for each change of text, you pay the page multiple times in the long term.

Hidden Traps of Cheap Offers

The 200 KM page usually means: a template used by 500 other companies, without SEO, at a hosting that you do not control, without any support. Then pay again for a year – this time and for repairing damage. The most expensive page is the one you pay twice.
