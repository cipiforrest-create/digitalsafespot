Social networking: what you actually pay and what you get

Guide · Social Network (SMM) · July 2026.

Instagram and Facebook are the first impression of your business today — the customer looks at them often before the website. The profile that he published four months ago sends a message: "maybe we are not doing it anymore." The question is not whether you need a presence, but who will guide it — and what exactly you get for money.

What serious conduct involves (our definition)

  • Strategy: To whom we address, what we differ, what is the purpose of each publication before the first picture.
  • Contents: with us 12 posts + 4 Reels videos monthly, in your visual identity.
  • Short video (Reels): The format that algorithms currently push most – ~39% of marketers refer to it as priority #1 for 2026.
  • Community: answer to messages and comments. Request without answer 24h = customer with competition.
  • Analysis: every month, what grew, why, and what we changed.

How to identify "picture publishers"

Three red flags: (1) ask you nothing about business before offer, (2) measures of success by number of posts instead of number of queries, (3) all content are stock photos with quotes. Networks without goal are cost; networks with the goal are sales channel.

How much is it in BiH?

The market range is 300–1,500 KM monthly, depending on the volume. Our package begins Of 249 KM monthly — with Reels production and response to messages included, because without it management makes no sense. For salons, gastros and shops, networks + Automatic reviews are the strongest combination for local growth.
