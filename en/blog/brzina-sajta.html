Why the slow site quietly kills your sales (and how to score 90+)

Guide · Performance · July 2026.

Slow site is the most expensive problem you can't see. No one will complain to you — the visitor simply pushes back and goes to competition. Each extra second of loading costs up to 20% of visitorsand over half of mobile users leave the page that is loaded for more than three seconds.

Double damage: customers + Google

The speed does not only affect customer patience. Google openly says it as a rating factor (Core Web Vitals): two similar sites, faster goes above. It means that the slow site loses twice – and the visitors it has received, and those that it will never get due to a worse position.

Check in 60 seconds.

Open our free analysis (use real Google Lighthouse data) or Google's PageSpeed Insights. mobile The assessment — most of the visits are coming. Over 90 is excellent, 50-89 means you leave money on the table, it is urgent below 50.

Five most common (in frequency order)

  1. Unoptimised images — a photo of 4 MB where 80 KB of WebP is needed. Blame number 1 in 8 of 10 sites.
  2. Cheap, crowded hosting - server responds slowly before anything starts to load.
  3. Too many add-ons/scripts — each plugin, widget and "only one more tracking tool" slows down.
  4. No Cache — The site is built for each visitor from the beginning instead of serving a finished copy.
  5. Effects on themes - a topic that can do anything, so it's slow.
