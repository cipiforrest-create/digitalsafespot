Growth path · Automation

One signal.Five steps.

Follow a single enquiry through the system — from the moment someone looks for you to the moment they become a client — and see the workflows carrying it under the surface.

  1. Someone looks for a service you offer and comes across you — in search, on the map or in an AI answer.

  2. The site holds them: fast loading, a clear offer and one obvious next step.

  3. The assistant answers even at three in the morning, qualifies the need and collects the contact.

  4. The enquiry enters the CRM, the appointment is booked and the reminder goes out — without a single manual entry.

  5. The client signs, and the report shows how much went in and how much came back.

Scroll or hover through the steps. Tap a step for details.
