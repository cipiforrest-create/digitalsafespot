Cookie policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026. Optional analytics is declined by default, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files that your browser stores on your device. The website may also use similar technologies, such as localStorage, to remember settings. Some technologies are necessary for security and basic functionality, while analytics and marketing technologies require the user's choice.

2. Our categories

Essential settings

These settings enable the website to work, keep it secure and remember your privacy choice. They aren't used to build a marketing profile and can't be switched off in our panel because they're needed for basic functionality.

Analytics and measurement

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 may set first-party cookies to distinguish users and sessions and to measure pages visited, devices, traffic sources and content performance. The data is used to improve the website and for reporting.

Advertising and personalisation

Consent Mode v2 passes on your choice for ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization and analytics_storage. These categories are declined by default. If no active ad tags are on the page, giving consent does not by itself start a new ad campaign.

4. Google Consent Mode v2

Before you choose, we set the state denied for analytics and ad storage. After you choose, a status update is sent. In certain configurations Google tags may receive limited signals without identifying cookies before consent; analytics cookies are not allowed until the user accepts them.

5. How to change your choice

At the bottom of every page, click . You can accept all optional categories, use essential settings only, or save a selected setting. Withdrawing consent applies to future processing.

You can also delete cookies in your browser settings. Clearing local storage removes the saved choice and the banner will appear again.
