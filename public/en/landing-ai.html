Frequently asked questions

What does the AI assistant actually do?

It answers questions 24/7 on your site and messaging channels, qualifies enquiries and collects contact details. When it does not know the answer, or the customer asks, it hands the conversation over to a person.

Where does the assistant get information about my business?

From your own content and in your tone of voice — pricing, services, opening hours, common questions. It does not invent answers about things you have not given it.
