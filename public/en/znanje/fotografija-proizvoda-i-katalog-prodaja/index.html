SHORT ANSWER

Online buyers cannot hold a product in their hands, so photography and catalogue clarity directly shape trust and conversion. A strong visual system pairs consistent framing (hero angle, material close-up and scale shot) with WebP compression, explicit width/height attributes for Core Web Vitals and descriptive alt text for Google Images.

Visual standards for web shops, catalogues and Image SEO

Under Google Search Central: Image SEO Best Practices, product visuals belong in standard HTML <img> tags using WebP, descriptive filenames and clear alt text, with explicit dimensions to keep CLS < 0.1 (Core Web Vitals guide, Product photography, Catalogue design).

Frequently asked questions

How many photos should each product have in an online store?

At least three to five: a clean hero shot, side/back angle, material close-up and an in-use scale photo.

Is physical printing included in digital catalogue design?

No. Design and print-ready preparation are quoted separately from physical printing, print runs and print vendors.
