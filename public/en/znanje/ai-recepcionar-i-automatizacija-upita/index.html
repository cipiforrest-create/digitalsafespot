SHORT ANSWER

An AI receptionist and X Lead Desk solve the biggest source of lost leads: inquiries arriving evenings or weekends that wait until the next day. The workflow greets the visitor, asks three to four qualification questions, prepares a structured summary and syncs it to your CRM or email with retry checks and human confirmation.

End-to-end lead handoff with human confirmation

An accepted webhook is not proof that a CRM record succeeded. X Lead Desk and AI Receptionist use unique handoff IDs, retry rules and separate channel verification across web, phone and calendar, keeping sensitive actions under human approval per OWASP LLM06 (Automation Starter from 349 KM (≈ €178)).

Frequently asked questions

Can the AI receptionist book calendar appointments automatically?

Yes, once a compatible calendar, availability rules and booking confirmation are connected and tested; no slot is booked until the calendar confirms it.
