Website conversion optimization (CRO): how to turn existing traffic into leads and orders

SHORT ANSWER

Conversion rate optimization (CRO) removes the friction that stops visitors from calling, messaging or ordering. The biggest gains usually come from four fixes: clear offer and starting price within five seconds, visible proof of work, a short 3–4 field mobile form and fast lead capture via WhatsApp or an AI assistant.

Where visitors drop off and high-impact CRO fixes

Before raising ad budgets, check your landing page on mobile data: visitors decide within five seconds whether your offer, pricing range and next step are clear. Frequently asked questions

Should a contact form be short or detailed?

For initial inquiries, a short 3–4 field form almost always converts better on mobile; extra qualification details can follow via WhatsApp, phone or guided AI chat.
