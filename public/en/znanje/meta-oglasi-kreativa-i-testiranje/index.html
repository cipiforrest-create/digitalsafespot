Meta Ads creative and testing structure: how to lower cost per inquiry on Instagram and Facebook

SHORT ANSWER

In 2026, creative does more targeting work on Instagram and Facebook than narrow interest stacking. The lowest cost per inquiry comes from testing three to five distinct message angles (problem, product demo, proof, direct offer) inside a stable campaign structure paired with Meta Conversions API and a focused landing page.

Creative-first testing without resetting the learning phase

Under Meta Business Help: Conversions API, Meta’s delivery system needs stable, deduplicated conversion signals to exit learning. Test 3–5 genuinely different hooks rather than tiny color tweaks, avoid daily budget edits during the first 5–7 days and send clicks to a matching landing page (from 199 KM (≈ €102)) (Meta Ads service).

Frequently asked questions

How many creatives should an active Meta campaign run?

Three to five distinct creatives (mixing short vertical video and clear static cards) are enough for most SMEs, refreshed when frequency rises.

How much does Meta Ads management cost at Digital X?

Growth Campaigns start at 249 KM (≈ €127) a month plus ad spend paid directly to Meta.
