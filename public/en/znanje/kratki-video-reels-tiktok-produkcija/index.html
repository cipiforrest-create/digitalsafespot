SHORT ANSWER

The most sustainable way for an SME to publish Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts is one planned monthly Content Day instead of rushed daily filming. With 8–12 scripted buyer questions, a clear 3-second visual hook and edited captions for muted viewing, one shoot feeds both organic channels and paid ads.

Four-phase Content Day workflow and silent-first captions

Batch filming A-roll answers and B-roll product demos in half a day keeps quality consistent across channels. Following W3C WAI: Captions/Subtitles, always review and correct auto-generated captions so silent mobile viewers understand the offer immediately (Product video, Content System from 249 KM (≈ €127)/mo).

How many short videos per month does a small business need?

Two to three quality posts per week (including 4–8 short videos a month) are enough when topics answer real buyer questions.
