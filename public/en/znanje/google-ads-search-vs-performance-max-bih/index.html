Google Ads Search vs. Performance Max: which campaign brings higher-quality leads in BiH

SHORT ANSWER

For most service businesses in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Google Search is the safer starting point because it targets buyers actively searching for your service and city with full keyword and negative-term control. Performance Max works best for online stores or scaling once you have verified conversion signals and strong creative assets.

Search vs. Performance Max and four budget-protection controls

Under Google Ads Help: About Performance Max, PMax serves ads across Search, Maps, YouTube, Display, Discover and Gmail from one campaign. Pair Search campaigns with a focused landing page (from 199 KM (≈ €102)), verified GA4 key events, strict city/country geo-targeting and weekly negative keyword audits (Google Ads service).

Frequently asked questions

Is Performance Max better than Search for a small service business in BiH?

For most local and B2B services on modest budgets, Search is safer because every click comes from active search intent. Performance Max fits e-commerce feeds or scaling after stable conversions.

How much does Google Ads management cost at Digital X?

Growth Campaigns management starts at 249 KM (≈ €127) a month, with ad spend paid directly to Google separately.
