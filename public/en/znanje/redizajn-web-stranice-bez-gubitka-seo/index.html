SHORT ANSWER

A website redesign loses Google rankings when URLs change without 301 redirects, service copy and H1 headings are stripped out, or a staging noindex tag leaks into production. A safe redesign starts with a full URL inventory, a 1:1 301 redirect map, JSON-LD entity parity and immediate Search Console monitoring after launch.

Four-phase SEO-safe redesign workflow

Under Google Search Central: Site Moves with URL Changes, any changed URL without a permanent redirect is treated as a brand-new page with zero history. Export all indexed URLs, preserve winning slugs, map changed routes 1:1 via single-hop 301/308 redirects, block staging cleanly and submit your updated sitemap plus IndexNow on launch day (Web design packages).

Frequently asked questions

Can we redirect all old URLs to the homepage during a redesign?

No. Redirecting unrelated old pages to the homepage is often treated as a soft 404 and fails to transfer page-level relevance.
