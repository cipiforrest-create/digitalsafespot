SHORT ANSWER

A RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) website chatbot does not guess answers from general model memory; before replying, it retrieves facts strictly from your approved price lists, service scopes and FAQs. Combined with allowlisted public actions and summary confirmation before submission, it answers in five languages without inventing discounts or timelines.

How RAG and OWASP LLM guardrails prevent hallucinations

Under OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, business assistants must mitigate prompt injection (LLM01), sensitive data disclosure (LLM02) and excessive agency (LLM06). Our AI Sales Assistant (from 490 KM (≈ €251)) answers strictly from approved public sources, discloses that it is an AI guide and hands complex requests to your team (try AI Concierge).

Frequently asked questions

Can a RAG chatbot invent prices or unapproved discounts?

No, when properly grounded in your approved catalogue and response rules, it quotes only published starting prices and notes that final scope is confirmed in writing.

How long does setup take on an existing website?

Basic setup takes 5 to 10 business days after receiving your approved pricing, service descriptions and FAQs.
