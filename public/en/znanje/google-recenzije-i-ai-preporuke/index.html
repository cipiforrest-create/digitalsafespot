SHORT ANSWER

Google reviews influence local visibility and AI recommendations through four signals: total volume and rating, steady recency, written context naming the service and city, and consistent owner replies. Buying fake reviews or filtering unhappy customers (review gating) violates Google policies and risks review removal or profile suspension.

Why written review context beats bare 5-star ratings

A five-star rating with no text confirms only a score, whereas a written review mentioning the specific service and city gives semantic relevance to both Google Maps and AI assistants (Chen et al., 2025). Under Google user-contributed content policies, ask every real customer honestly after service completion without incentives or gating (Review Engine from 199 KM (≈ €102)).
