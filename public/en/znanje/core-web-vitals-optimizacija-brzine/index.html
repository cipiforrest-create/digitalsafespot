SHORT ANSWER

Core Web Vitals are Google’s three real-user experience metrics: LCP (main content loading ≤ 2.5s), INP (interaction responsiveness < 200ms, which replaced FID) and CLS (visual stability < 0.1). On most business websites, slow scores come from uncompressed hero images, render-blocking CSS/JS and unreserved font or media space rather than hosting alone.

Core Web Vitals thresholds and primary fixes in 2026

Under Google Search Central: Core Web Vitals, Google evaluates real-user field performance at the 75th percentile across mobile and desktop. Passing all three thresholds supports both classic rankings and eligibility for Google AI Overviews, while Interaction to Next Paint (INP) measures responsiveness across every tap and click.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 100 PageSpeed Insights score required for SEO?

No. Google evaluates real-user Core Web Vitals thresholds (LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1) rather than a lab score of 100, though lab diagnostics pinpoint what slows mobile devices down.
