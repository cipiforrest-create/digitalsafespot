SHORT ANSWER

A successful online store in Bosnia and Herzegovina combines cash-on-delivery (still widely used for domestic orders) with card checkout via a local bank or regional payment processor. Shoppers abandon carts mainly due to surprise shipping fees, forced account creation and unclear return terms, while Google expects OfferShippingDetails and MerchantReturnPolicy schema matching visible pages.

Card checkout, cash-on-delivery and Merchant schema in BiH

Offering both card payments and cash-on-delivery (pouzeće) captures domestic buyers as well as diaspora orders. Under Google Search Central: Merchant Listing guidelines, structured OfferShippingDetails and MerchantReturnPolicy data should match your visible shipping and return policy pages (E-commerce development from 2,490 KM (≈ €1,273)).

Frequently asked questions

Is online card payment mandatory for a web shop in BiH?

No, if you offer cash-on-delivery or bank transfer, though card checkout improves conversion for diaspora buyers and reduces uncollected COD parcels.
