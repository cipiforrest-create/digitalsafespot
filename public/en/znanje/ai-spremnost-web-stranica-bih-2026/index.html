SHORT ANSWER

Appearing in Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity requires no special AI files or experimental schema, but technical crawlability, Core Web Vitals (LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1), standalone 40–80 word passages for query fan-out and verifiable citations. Peer-reviewed KDD 2024 research shows statistics, quotes and citations lift generative visibility by up to 40%.

X Signal 2026 benchmark: 78 BiH business sites & our 119-page corpus

On 22 November 2026, we ran our deterministic X Signal analyzer across 95 public business websites in Bosnia and Herzegovina (IT, banking, telecom, retail/e-commerce, healthcare, manufacturing and hospitality), of which 78 returned readable HTTP 200 HTML. While 100% used HTTPS and 98.7% declared <html lang>, only 43.6% had a single H1, 50.0% published Organization/LocalBusiness schema, 30.8% declared explicit max-snippet:-1, 44.9% had two or more 30–90 word extractable passages and 0.0% combined a visible byline with structured author provenance (compared with 5.9%, 47.1% and 14.3% on Digital X’s own 119-page corpus before our November 2026 upgrade).
