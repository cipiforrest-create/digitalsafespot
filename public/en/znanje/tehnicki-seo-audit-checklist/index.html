SHORT ANSWER

A 15-minute technical SEO audit checks ten signals: canonical host without redirect loops, robots.txt, XML sitemap, single H1 heading, meta description, self-canonical tag, Core Web Vitals, valid Organization/LocalBusiness JSON-LD, max-snippet:-1 and standalone 40–80 word passages. Across 78 scanned BiH business sites, 56.4% had H1 errors and 50.0% lacked entity schema.

What our 78-site BiH X Signal scan revealed

On 22 November 2026, we scanned 95 public BiH business websites (78 returned readable HTTP 200 HTML) using X Signal. While 100% used HTTPS and 98.7% declared <html lang>, 56.4% failed the single-H1 check, 50.0% lacked Organization/LocalBusiness schema, 69.2% omitted explicit max-snippet:-1 and 55.1% lacked two or more 30–90 word passages (Google SEO Starter Guide).
