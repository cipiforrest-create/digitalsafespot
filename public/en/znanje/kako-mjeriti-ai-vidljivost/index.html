GUIDE · AI VISIBILITY MEASUREMENT

How to measure brand visibility in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode

Prepared and reviewed by: Digital X SEO & Web Team · Editorial methodology · updated 22 Nov 2026

  1. Fixed 20–30 prompt tracking vs. fake visibility scores
  2. Connecting AI citations to GSC, GA4 and Core Updates

SHORT ANSWER

AI visibility is not measured by an invented 0–100 score, but by testing a fixed set of 20–30 buyer prompts monthly across Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. Each entry logs the date, brand mention and exact cited URL, paired with Search Console and GA4 conversions while waiting 7 full days after any Google Core Update.

Fixed 20–30 prompt tracking vs. fake visibility scores

Generative answers vary by context, location and language, so no agency can guarantee a fixed rank in ChatGPT. Instead, track a fixed 20–30 prompt set across Bosnian, English and German covering informational, pricing, local Sarajevo/BiH and DACH queries, distinguishing unlinked brand mentions from clickable URL citations (Chen et al., 2025).

Connecting AI citations to GSC, GA4 and Core Updates

Pair monthly prompt logs with Search Console query/page clicks and GA4 referral traffic plus form, WhatsApp and phone conversions. Following Google Core Updates guidance, wait at least seven full days after rollout completion before comparing week-over-week data (Digital X editorial methodology).

Frequently asked questions

How often should we test our AI prompt set?

Monthly across a fixed 20–30 prompt set, plus 7–14 days after major content updates or a completed Google Core Update.

Do you charge extra per tracked AI prompt?

No. Fixed prompt tracking is part of transparent SEO · GEO · AEO reporting without per-prompt surcharges (/en/usluge/seo-aeo-geo-cijena/).
