SEO, AEO and GEO: what is the difference and what does your business need

SHORT ANSWER

SEO helps Google understand and rank your pages in search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures content into concise 40–80 word answers for direct extraction. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) builds verifiable facts, citations and external mentions so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity cite your brand accurately.

Three disciplines in one table

SEO, AEO and GEO are three layers of the same visibility system rather than separate budgets. Technical crawlability supports direct answer extraction, while verifiable facts and third-party mentions strengthen generative citations.

What Google officially requires for AI Overviews and AI Mode

Official Google Search Central: AI Features documentation confirms that no special Schema.org types or llms.txt files are required for AI Overviews or AI Mode. Standard Search eligibility, Core Web Vitals, clean HTML text and JSON-LD matching visible content govern inclusion, while KDD 2024 research (Aggarwal et al.) shows verifiable statistics and citations lift generative visibility by up to 40%.

Frequently asked questions

Do AEO and GEO replace classic SEO?

No. They build directly on technical SEO, crawlable HTML and helpful content.

Is llms.txt required for Google AI Overviews?

No. Google Search Central states no special AI text files are needed; llms.txt is optional for third-party AI tools.
