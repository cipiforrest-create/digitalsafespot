Multi-city local SEO in Bosnia: how to rank without doorway spam risk

SHORT ANSWER

Businesses serving multiple cities in Bosnia and Herzegovina should not publish ten identical pages that only swap the city name, because Google classifies cloned city templates as doorway spam. Publish a dedicated city page only where you have a real office or team, and cover other cities through one strong service hub, areaServed JSON-LD and real project proof.

Why cloned city pages violate Google Spam Policies

Under Google Spam Policies for doorway pages, thin pages created solely to capture city-name searches with identical pricing tables and FAQs dilute domain authority and risk ranking drops. Consolidate thin city clones via permanent 301/308 redirects into your main service hub (Local SEO Sarajevo).

Frequently asked questions

Does consolidating thin city pages hurt organic traffic?

When legacy city URLs permanently redirect (301/308) to a strong service hub and are removed from the sitemap, link equity consolidates cleanly without doorway risk.

How can clients in other cities find us without a local branch?

Through a comprehensive service hub declaring BiH/DACH remote delivery, areaServed JSON-LD, client reviews and city-targeted Google Ads.
