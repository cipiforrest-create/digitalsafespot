SHORT ANSWER

In 2026, the highest-value Schema.org types for SEO and AI search are connected Organization, LocalBusiness, Service with Offer pricing, Article, ProfilePage and BreadcrumbList entities. Google restricted FAQPage rich results to authoritative government and health sites in August 2023 and retired seven types in June 2025 including ClaimReview, CourseInfo and SpecialAnnouncement.

Active Schema.org entity graph vs. retired 2025 types

Connected JSON-LD helps search engines and AI assistants verify your legal entity, location, pricing and editorial provenance without guessing. In our 78-site BiH benchmark, 50.0% of business websites lacked Organization or LocalBusiness schema, while Google Structured Data Policies require strict parity with visible page text.

Three rules for error-free JSON-LD

Link every Service, Offer and Article back to one canonical #organization node via @id, match every BAM/EUR price and claim verbatim to visible HTML, and populate sameAs only with verified profiles such as Google Business Profile, YouTube, Instagram, Threads and GitHub (Digital X facts).

Frequently asked questions

Does FAQPage schema still help AI search without Google rich results?

Visible HTML FAQ sections help users and LLM passage extraction, while FAQPage JSON-LD remains valid when matching visible text—even though commercial sites no longer receive FAQ rich snippets in Google Search.

Which seven Schema types did Google retire in June 2025?

BookActions, CourseInfo, ClaimReview, EstimatedSalary, LearningVideo, SpecialAnnouncement and VehicleListing.
