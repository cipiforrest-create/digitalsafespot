Do you need llms.txt in 2026: what Google, ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity actually read

SHORT ANSWER

Google Search Central explicitly confirms that llms.txt is not required for Google AI Overviews or AI Mode. Google relies on crawlable HTML, Core Web Vitals, max-snippet:-1 and accurate JSON-LD. Files like llms.txt and llms-full.txt are optional plain-text summaries for third-party AI tools, paired with open robots.txt rules and IndexNow.

What llms.txt is and why Google Search does not require it

The /llms.txt proposal (llmstxt.org) provides a clean Markdown summary of key pages and pricing at the domain root. However, Google Search Central: AI Features confirms no special AI text files are needed for AI Overviews or AI Mode.

robots.txt, IndexNow and when llms.txt makes sense

Ensure robots.txt allows Googlebot, Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot on public pages, and trigger IndexNow after deploys so Bing and ChatGPT Search pick up price updates immediately. Generate llms.txt and llms-full.txt from the same catalogue as your HTML pages so facts never drift.

Frequently asked questions

Will my site miss Google AI Overviews without llms.txt?

No. Google Search Central confirms llms.txt is not required for AI Overviews or AI Mode; focus on crawlable HTML, Core Web Vitals and JSON-LD.

Should robots.txt allow GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot?

Yes, if you want ChatGPT Search and AI assistants to discover and cite your public services and current pricing.
