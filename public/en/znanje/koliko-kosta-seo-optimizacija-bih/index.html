SHORT ANSWER

Monthly SEO in Bosnia and Herzegovina typically costs between 249 KM (≈ €127) and 800 KM (≈ €409) a month for small and mid-sized businesses, while larger online shops and multilingual DACH projects range from 1,000 KM (≈ €511) to 2,500+ KM a month. One-off speed or technical fixes start at 99 KM (≈ €51). At Digital X, SEO · GEO · AEO starts at 249 KM (≈ €127) a month.

Frequently asked questions

At Digital X, monthly SEO · GEO · AEO covering technical SEO, local visibility, answer structure and reporting starts at 249 KM (≈ €127) a month.

Should I invest in SEO or Google Ads first?

If you need enquiries immediately, start with Google Ads and a landing page while building SEO/AEO/GEO in parallel to lower long-term acquisition cost.
