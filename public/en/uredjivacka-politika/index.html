SHORT ANSWER

Every guide and technical recommendation published by Digital X undergoes human editorial and engineering review in Sarajevo. Claims are grounded in official Google Search Central documentation, peer-reviewed GEO research and reproducible X Signal HTML checks, with zero fabricated statistics, fake reviews or ranking guarantees.

Who prepares and reviews content

Content is prepared and reviewed by web engineering, technical SEO/GEO/AEO and AI automation specialists within the Digital X team in Sarajevo (Stupska bb). We never publish unreviewed mass-generated pages that violate Google Spam Policies.

How we verify claims and evaluate Core Updates

Technical guidance follows Google Search Central: AI Features and Core Web Vitals. After a Google Core Update finishes rolling out, we wait at least one full week before comparing Search Console data against the pre-rollout week (Google Core Updates).

Frequently asked questions

Does Digital X use AI tools when drafting guides?

AI tools may assist with structuring drafts, but every published price, technical claim and legal note is verified by human editors before publication.

Why do you not promise guaranteed rankings or traffic percentages?

Search rankings and AI recommendations depend on competition and third-party systems. We publish verifiable prices, methodology and measurable technical findings only.
