When Excel and notebooks are no longer enough.

Applications · 5 min

Guide · Applications · July 2026.

Every growth business comes to the same point: reservations are kept in notebooks, supplies in Excel, work orders in the Viber Group — and every day something "curve". your processes, instead of you bending your processes around someone else's finished tool.

How do you know you need it?

  • The same information is written in two or three places (and versions do not match)
  • The answer to "how much did we charge this month?" requires half a day digging.
  • The finished tools you tried are doing 70% of the work, and the 30% of them are killing you.
  • Employees spend hours on something the system would do alone

What the process looks like (no slang)

  1. Writing process (1 week): We sit down and pass through the work that really runs — who writes what, where it goes.
  2. Cyclic prototype: before any line of code you see screens and click through the application. This changes everything that is wrong – while the change is free.
  3. Phase development: First core that solves the biggest pain (MVP), then upgrades. You do not wait 6 months for the "big day" – use the system after the first phase.
  4. Training and support: The team learns how to work in the app, and we stay for the finishing.

How much is it?

On the BiH market, applications cost about 5,000 KM for simple systems up to 35,000+ KM for complex solutions. We have a simpler web application (reservations, records, internal orders) starting well below that threshold — because we build smartly, in stages, and we charge what you really use. One database covers the web, Android and iOS, which also cuts the price of development and maintenance costs.

Best first step: describe the process that costs you most time. You get the assessment and the proposal of the phase for free — so decide for yourself whether it pays off. Schedule conversation →
