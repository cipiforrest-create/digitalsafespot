Content marketing

Selling that doesn't feel like selling.

010203
Content marketingYour next step

Content · 4 min

Guide · Content · July 2026

People don't buy from people they don't know — and they don't like being sold to either. Content marketing solves both: instead of "buy from us", you help — you write a guide, answer a question, explain prices. The reader gets value, you get trust. And trust is the only thing that comes before a purchase.

What it looks like in practice

  • Blog guides: "How much does X cost", "How to choose Y", "5 mistakes with Z" — exactly what your customers google at 2 a.m. (You're reading an example right now.)
  • Service pages that sell: not a list, but a story: problem → solution → proof → price → call to action.
  • PR articles: on news portals — they build authority and earn links that Google rewards.
  • Email content: nurtures contacts who are "still thinking" until they're ready to buy.

Why this is an investment, not a cost

An ad works while you pay. A good article works for years: a guide written today still brings in customers in 2029 — for free, every day, while you sleep. And it's double fuel: the same article feeds both Google (SEO) and AI search (AEO) — ChatGPT and Perplexity quote exactly the content that answers questions clearly and specifically, with numbers.

The biggest mistake

Writing about yourself ("our history", "our team", "our mission") instead of your customers' problems. Nobody googles your mission. They google their pain — be the best answer to it.
