What can be automated

Six automations that pay for themselves fastest.

010203
What can be automatedYour next step

Automation · 5 min

Guide · AI automation · July 2026

Most small business owners spend 10–30 hours a month on tasks a machine does better: reminding, retyping, sending, copying from one spreadsheet to another. In 2026 automation is no longer a corporate luxury — it's set up in a week and costs less than one working day of an employee.

Six automations that pay for themselves fastest

  1. AI chatbot (from 490 KM + 149 KM/month): answers 80% of customer questions 24/7 — detailed guide here.
  2. Appointment reminders (from 349 KM + 249 KM/month): the customer books online and the system sends a WhatsApp/SMS reminder the day before. Salons and clinics typically halve missed appointments this way.
  3. Review collector (from 199 KM + 249 KM/month): an automatic message after the service with a link to leave a Google review — guide here.
  4. Email sequences (from 249 KM): a new enquiry automatically receives a series of messages that guide it towards a purchase. A lead that gets a reply in the first minute is worth many times more than one that waits until tomorrow.
  5. Automatic reports: on the first of the month an overview arrives by itself: visits, enquiries, sales — in plain language, without digging through tools.
  6. Custom internal processes: the quote builds itself from the price list, the invoice sends itself, the spreadsheet fills itself in. This is where the biggest savings are — and the biggest surprises about what's possible.

What the roll-out looks like

No revolution: (1) we pick the one process that hurts most, (2) set up the automation in 5–7 days, (3) measure the savings for a month, (4) only then expand. One automation that works quietly and reliably is worth more than ten "impressive" ones nobody uses.

The real maths

An employee who manually sends reminders, retypes enquiries and compiles reports spends about 15 hours a month on it. At 15 KM/h that's 225 KM every month — forever. Automation: a one-off 199–490 KM + about 30 KM a month. It pays for itself in the first month, not the first year.
