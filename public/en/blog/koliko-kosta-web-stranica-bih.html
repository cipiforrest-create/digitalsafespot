How much does a website cost in Bosnia and Herzegovina in 2026?

A realistic guide to prices — and how not to pay twice.

Guide · Web · updated July 2026

Short answer: a professional brochure website in Bosnia and Herzegovina costs 500–1,800 KM, an online store from 3,000 KM upwards, and a custom web app from 5,000 KM to 35,000+ KM. The price difference isn't about who charges what — it's about what's included.

What you get for each price range

  • Up to 500 KM: a simple brochure website (up to 5 subpages, contact form, mobile design). For lawyers, clinics, trades and salons this is quite enough — if it's done properly: fast, secure and with basic SEO.
  • 800–1,800 KM: larger websites with more content, multiple languages, a blog, more advanced SEO set-up and custom design.
  • From 3,000 KM: an online store — products, payments, shipping, invoices. You're paying for the process set-up too, not just the look.
  • From 5,000 KM: custom apps (booking systems, internal records, portals).

Five questions to ask EVERY provider

  1. How fast does the site load on a phone? Look for a Google score of 85+. A slow page starts losing visitors within the first few seconds.
  2. Is SEO included or extra? A website without basic SEO is a shop window in the basement.
  3. Who owns the website and the domain? Always you. If the agency keeps ownership — walk away.
  4. What happens after launch? Without updates and backups, the site is a ticking clock. Maintenance realistically costs 29–150 KM a month.
  5. Can I edit the content myself? If you pay a developer for every text change, you end up paying for the site several times over.

The hidden traps of cheap offers

A 200 KM website usually means: a template used by 500 other businesses, no SEO, hosting you don't control and no support at all. A year later you pay again — this time to repair the damage too. The most expensive website is the one you pay for twice.
