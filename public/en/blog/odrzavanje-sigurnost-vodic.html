Maintenance: a 49 KM insurance policy

What happens to a website nobody looks after.

Maintenance · 4 min

Guide · Maintenance & security · July 2026

A website without maintenance is a car without servicing: it runs — until it stops. And it always stops at the worst moment: mid-season, the day before a campaign, on a Saturday night. This guide explains what really happens "under the hood" and why maintenance is the cheapest line in your digital budget.

What happens to a website nobody looks after

  • Security holes: the system and plugins age; hacker bots automatically scan thousands of sites looking for known holes. They don't "pick" you — they find you.
  • A hacked site = triple damage: lost sales + a Google warning that "this site may be harmful" (it kills trust for months) + a clean-up bill bigger than years of maintenance.
  • Silent failures: the form stops sending enquiries and nobody notices for weeks. How many enquiries did you lose? You'll never know.

What our maintenance includes (from 49 KM/month)

  1. Updates: system, plugins and security patches — regularly, checking that nothing breaks.
  2. Daily backup: whatever happens, the site is back in an hour — not in a week.
  3. Monitoring 24/7: if the site goes down, we know before you do — and before your customers.
  4. Small changes: changing a price, opening hours or photos — at no extra charge.

How much does it cost in Bosnia and Herzegovina?

The market range is 30–150+ KM a month. We have three packages: 49 KM a month for basic technical maintenance of the site, 149 KM for everything in the first package plus upkeep of the AI digital assistant, and 249 KM for everything in the first two packages plus upkeep of automations. For online shops, where an hour of downtime means lost orders, we recommend a higher package with priority support.

A 10-second test: do you know when your website was last backed up? If the answer is "I don't know" — get in touch today, before that answer starts to matter.
